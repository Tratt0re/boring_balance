# Privacy Policy

## Your data stays yours.

Boring Balance is a free, open-source, local-first personal finance app built with privacy in mind.

We believe managing your money shouldn't mean giving up control of your data.

That's why Boring Balance works without accounts, subscriptions, tracking, or cloud servers. Your financial information stays on your device, under your control.

## 1. What data do we collect?

**None.**

Boring Balance does not collect, store, transmit, or share your personal or financial information with its developers.

There are:

- No user accounts or registrations.
- No analytics, telemetry, or usage tracking.
- No advertisements or profiling.
- No third-party tracking SDKs.
- No financial data sent to our servers.

We don't know who you are, how much money you have, or how you use the app. And we don't need to.

## 2. Where is your data stored?

All your financial data is stored locally on your device in a SQLite database.

Boring Balance reads and processes this information only to provide the features you use, such as tracking transactions, managing budgets, and reviewing your finances.

There is no Boring Balance cloud service or hosted database.

Your information remains accessible to you, without depending on our infrastructure.

## 3. Backups, exports, and synchronization

Boring Balance lets you create backups, export data, and synchronize files through folders you choose.

These features are entirely under your control.

If you choose a folder connected to a third-party service, such as a cloud storage provider, your files may be synchronized by that service according to its own privacy policy.

Boring Balance does not operate or manage these services and does not receive copies of your data.

You are responsible for choosing where your files are stored, maintaining backups, and keeping them secure.

## 4. Internet connections and updates

Boring Balance works offline and does not require an internet connection for everyday use.

The app may connect to GitHub's public API to check whether a newer version is available.

This request does not include your financial information, account details, or app usage data. However, GitHub may receive technical connection information, such as your IP address, as part of a standard web request.

Updates are never downloaded or installed automatically. You decide whether to update the app.

## 5. Data control and deletion

You remain in control of your information.

You can manage, export, and delete your financial records using the application's features.

Because Boring Balance does not collect or remotely store your data, we cannot access, restore, or delete it on your behalf.

Removing the application may not automatically remove local databases, backups, or exported files. These can be deleted separately from your device or chosen storage locations.

## 6. Open source and transparency

Boring Balance is open source.

Its code is publicly available, so anyone can inspect how the application works and verify how data is handled.

You don't have to take our word for it.

You can explore the source code on [GitHub](https://github.com/Tratt0re/boring_balance).

## 7. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the application's functionality or privacy practices change.

Any changes will be published on the official Boring Balance website.

## 8. Contact

For privacy-related questions, feedback, or concerns, you can open an issue on our [GitHub repository](https://github.com/Tratt0re/boring_balance).

You can also visit [boringbalance.com](https://boringbalance.com) for more information.
